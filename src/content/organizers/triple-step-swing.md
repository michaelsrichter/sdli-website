Triple Step Swing is Carol Fraser's swing dance school near Plainview. It teaches Lindy Hop and other classic swing styles, runs monthly DJ swing dances, and keeps a calendar of swing events around Long Island and New York City. Carol also teaches regularly at SDLI.
