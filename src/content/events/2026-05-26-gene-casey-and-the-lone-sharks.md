7:30 PM ADVANCED Swing Dance Lesson with Carol Fraser

Hit the floor as Gene Casey and the Lone Sharks bring their electrifying sound-perfect for nonstop dancing!
Whether you're ready to level up your moves or just want to ride the rhythm all night long, this is the place to be. Expect great music, vibrant energy, and a packed dance floor.
