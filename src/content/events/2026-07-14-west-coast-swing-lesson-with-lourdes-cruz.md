Ready to shake, slide, and swing your way into the night? Join us for an evening built around rhythm, connection, and pure dance joy!
7:30 PM | West Coast Swing Lesson
Learn the smooth, bouncy, and incredibly fun fundamentals of West Coast Swing with Lourdas Cruz. Whether you're picking up your first step or polishing your technique, this lesson is designed to get you on the floor and feeling confident.
8-10PM | Social Swing Dance
Put your moves to the test! Our DJ will spin a high-energy mix of classic swing, jazz, blues, and modern grooves to keep the dance floor buzzing.
&#9989; Beginners welcome! No experience? Perfect. We'll get you moving from minute one.
&#9989; No partner? No problem! Come solo, bring a friend, or show up with your crew. We'll make sure everyone has someone to dance with.
&#9989; Zero pressure, maximum fun. This is about connection, rhythm, and letting loose.
