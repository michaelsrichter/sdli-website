Join a friendly group of people every Tuesday evening for a mix of East Coast Swing, Savoy Lindy Hop, Hollywood Lindy, West Coast Swing, Balboa, Collegiate Shag and some ballroom dancing.

Every night starts with a swing lesson at 7:30 PM to get you on the floor. Social dancing with a DJ or a live band follows from 8 to 10 PM. Singles and beginners are welcome, and no partner is necessary.

Some Tuesdays are something special, such as pizza nights, guest teachers and live band nights. Check each date for details.
