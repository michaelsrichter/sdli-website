The Huntington Moose Lodge (also called "The Lodge," Huntington Moose 318) in Greenlawn is the home of SDLI's weekly Tuesday night swing dances.

## What to expect

- A spacious hall with a wood dance floor and a big stage for live bands.
- The bar is in a separate side room, so the dance floor stays roomy.
- Clean restrooms and a friendly crowd of SDLI regulars who are happy to dance with newcomers.

The Lodge phone number above is for the venue itself. For questions about SDLI dances, call the SDLI 24-hour Dance Hotline at (631) 476-3707.
