## How much does it cost?

Membership is **$12 per person per year**.

## When does membership run?

The membership year runs from **October 1 to September 30**. Everyone renews at the same time each year, so it is easy to remember when your membership ends.

## How do I join?

The best way to join is in person at any SDLI dance. Fill out a membership form at the door and pay your $12 dues. You will get your membership card right away.
