## What happens on a Tuesday night

1. **7:30 PM: Swing lesson.** A teacher walks the whole group through basic steps. Partners rotate, so you will practice with several people.
2. **8:00 PM: Social dancing.** A DJ or a live band plays swing music. Use the steps you just learned, or just watch and enjoy the music.
3. **10:00 PM: Last dance.**

## Do I need a partner?

No. Most people come on their own, and partners change during the lesson and all night long. Feel free to ask anyone to dance. "Yes" and "No, thank you" are both fine answers.

## What should I wear?

Wear comfortable clothes you can move in. Choose shoes that stay on your feet and let you turn easily. Smooth soles, such as leather or suede, work better than sticky rubber sneakers. You do not need vintage clothing, but some dancers enjoy dressing up.

## Which dances will I see?

You will see a mix of East Coast Swing, Lindy Hop, West Coast Swing, Balboa and some ballroom dancing. Beginners usually start with East Coast Swing, the simplest swing dance to learn.

## Tips for your first night

- Get there a few minutes early to pay at the door and settle in.
- Bring water, and take breaks when you need them.
- Everyone was a beginner once. Mistakes are part of the fun.
