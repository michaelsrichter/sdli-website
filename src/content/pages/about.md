## Who we are

Swing Dance Long Island, Inc. (SDLI) is run entirely by volunteers. We host a friendly swing dance every Tuesday night at the Huntington Moose Lodge in Greenlawn, with a lesson for beginners, DJ nights, live band nights and seasonal parties.

SDLI's online event archive goes back to October 2006. Over the years SDLI dances have featured dozens of bands and taken place at venues across Long Island.
